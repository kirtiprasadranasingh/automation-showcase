The Problem

Patients show up having eaten breakfast before a morning endoscopy or without a driver for sedation. The appointment is cancelled on the spot, and the scanner sits empty.

The Solution

The system reads the doctor's clinical notes, turns them into a simple, personalized WhatsApp checklist, and delivers it directly to the patient 24 hours prior.

The Result

Morning cancellations drop drastically. Clinics protect thousands in daily scan revenue, while nurses spend zero time making repetitive reminder calls.
