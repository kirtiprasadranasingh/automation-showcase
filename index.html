The Problem

One small mistake can take your service down

A single unsafe database query, or a table deleted by accident, can mean hours of downtime and unhappy customers. Teams usually catch these by reading code by hand. That is slow, and things get missed.

How It Works

The check runs automatically whenever code is submitted, with two clear paths:

Step 1

The code goes up for review as usual in your Git repository.

Risky changes: Blocked + fix suggested

The Problem

Forgotten preparation rules cause empty rooms and cancelled tests

Patients arrive having had breakfast when they needed to fast, or arrive alone when they needed someone to drive them home. The scan has to be cancelled on the spot. Rooms sit empty, nurses spend hours calling by phone, and patients go home frustrated.

The Solution

Plain English reminders delivered straight to the patient's phone

The moment an appointment is booked, the system reads the doctor's notes, creates a simple preparation checklist, and sends it over WhatsApp. Patients can tap quick confirmation buttons or ask routine preparation questions.

Note: This assistant provides appointment preparation instructions only, not medical advice.

1

Doctor notes the visit

The doctor books the test in the clinic system and notes simple preparation rules like fasting hours or driver requirements.

2

Automatic checklist

The system turns medical notes into clear bullet points that patients can easily read and follow without confusing jargon.

3

Patient confirms on phone

The patient reads the checklist on WhatsApp and confirms in one tap. Nurses see confirmed status in real time.
